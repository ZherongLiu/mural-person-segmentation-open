# Openness and Confidentiality Statement

This project follows an “open by default, confidential by exception” publication policy.

Public materials include project-owned code, trained project weights, de-identified annotations, the fixed test set,
non-sensitive experimental metrics, public operating documentation, and one low-resolution grayscale derivative for
each of the 25 temples.

The following materials are excluded from the public repository:

1. Precise locations, inspection routes, access-control details, and security information concerning heritage safety;
2. Personal names, contact details, identity documents, and unpublished collaboration information;
3. Color field photographs and complete high-resolution mural archives without public-distribution authorization;
4. Third-party materials restricted by copyright, license, or collaboration agreements;
5. Metadata that has not completed de-identification or rights review;
6. Information that may increase the risk of damage, looting, or unlawful reproduction of cultural heritage.

Public release does not constitute a waiver of rights. Code, models, annotations, and image pixels are governed by
their respective licenses and rights statements. Repository maintainers may remove files if security, privacy, or
rights risks are identified.

# 开放与保密声明

本项目遵循“默认开放、保密例外”的发布原则。

公开内容包括项目自有代码、训练后的项目权重、脱敏标注、固定测试集、非敏感实验指标、
公开操作文档，以及25座庙宇每庙1张灰度低分辨率派生样例。

以下内容不进入公开仓库：

1. 涉及文物安全的精确位置、巡查路线、门禁和安防信息；
2. 个人姓名、联系方式、身份证明和未公开合作信息；
3. 未取得公开授权的彩色实拍原图和完整高清壁画档案；
4. 第三方受版权、许可或合作协议限制的材料；
5. 未完成脱敏或权利审查的元数据；
6. 可能增加文物损害、盗掘或非法复制风险的信息。

公开不等于放弃权利。代码、模型、标注和图像像素分别适用各自的许可与权利说明。
仓库维护者可在发现安全、隐私或权利风险时撤下相关文件。
